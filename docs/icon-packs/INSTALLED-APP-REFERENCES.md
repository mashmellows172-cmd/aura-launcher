## Art direction gate

For each actual installed identity: inspect its current recognizable symbol; identify whether an authentic historical counterpart exists; mark either documented historical counterpart or modern reinterpretation. Material templates may share lighting and proportions, but should not turn all music, social or shopping apps into repeated generic symbols. Every output needs a visual review at32/42/60dp, on dark/light backgrounds, including silhouette without label.

## Coverage gate

Coverage denominator is the captured MAIN/LAUNCHER activity set for the current Android user, not all installed background packages and not a guessed popular-app list. Preserve component distinctions when multiple launcher entries differ. Inventory and capture output live outside the repository in work/icon-inventory. Exact mapping and original artwork can be committed without usage/account metadata.
